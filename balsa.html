Introduction to Structural Design · Spring 2026

The “Economic” Beam

Team: Corey Segal and Busra Nimet Yesil.

Image from the original team report.

PROJECT DOCUMENTATION

Read full report ↗

Original collaborative course report. Contributions are credited to the full team.

Challenge

Design a material-efficient balsa beam spanning 24 inches for a central load, using structural analysis to select member sizes and anticipate failure.

Engineering approach

Two trapezoidal side trusses formed a braced 3D box configuration. The team sized compression and tension chords from estimated axial forces, checked top-chord buckling, and reused balsa from a prior exercise.

Test results

The reported peak load was 90.56 lbf. The observed failure involved right-end joint separation and instability rather than the originally predicted center top-chord buckling. The team analyzed the end reaction and diagonal force to explain the discrepancy.
